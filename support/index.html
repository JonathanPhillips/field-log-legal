Field Log — Support

Private fishing & hunting log. Everything stays on your phone.

Backing up and moving to a new phone

Settings > Export backup creates a zip with all your entries, spots, species, and photos. Save it to Files or a drive you trust. On the new phone, install Field Log and use Settings > Import. Because the app has no account or cloud, this backup is the only way to move everything.

Free entries and unlocking

Field Log is free for your first 30 entries, with every feature included. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited entries. There is no subscription. Viewing, editing, exporting and deleting your entries always keep working, unlocked or not. Buying needs a signal, so unlock before you head somewhere remote; once unlocked, the app works fully offline.

New phone or reinstalled? Open Settings > Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Why isn't there a map?

Online maps send every area you look at to the map provider — that would reveal your spots. Field Log shows spots as a list or a plot around you instead. Offline maps you load from a file are planned.

Permissions

  • Location (while using): where entries happen, distance to spots.
  • Camera: photos for entries.
  • Add to Photos: only if "Save photos I take to my camera roll" is on.
  • Motion & Fitness: the barometer, for air pressure.

Everything works without them; you can enter locations by hand.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete All Data erases everything on the device. Export a backup first if you might want it back.
