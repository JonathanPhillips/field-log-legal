Field Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Field Log is a local-only app. Everything you record — entries, spots, photos, notes, and conditions — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app does not contact any server, and we do not operate one. Nothing you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location while it is open to record where an entry happened and to show distances to your spots. Locations are saved only on your device. The app never uses location in the background, and spots are drawn without online maps, so no map provider learns where you look.

Camera, photos & motion

Camera and photo access let you attach pictures to entries. The app keeps its own resized copy with location and device metadata removed; photos in your library are never changed. If "Save photos I take to my camera roll" is on, the copy saved there also has no location. Motion access is used only to read your phone's barometer for air pressure.

Backups

When you export a backup or CSV, the app hands the file to the system share sheet. Where it goes next — Files, email, a cloud drive — is your choice and is never seen by us. Backup files contain your spot locations, so share them carefully. Your device may also include app data in its own backups (iCloud or Google), under Apple's or Google's terms.

Purchases

Field Log is free for your first 30 entries; a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited entries. The purchase is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play, which is the only time the app talks to them. We receive no personal or payment information, and the unlock is remembered only on your device. Your entries are never part of it.

Analytics & tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind. We have no way to see how you use the app or what you store in it.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries, photos, spots, and species at any time, or erase everything at once from Settings > Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes all of its data, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue or use the contact listed on the App Store product page.
